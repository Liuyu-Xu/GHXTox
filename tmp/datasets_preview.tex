\noindent\textbf{Preview: Section 2.1 Datasets (text above, table below)}

We evaluate GHXTox on the benchmark datasets constructed by CAPTP [17] and
subsequently adopted by ToxMSRC [2]. Following the data construction protocol
of ToxMSRC [2], toxic peptide sequences were merged from three databases,
namely CSM-Toxin [18], ToxinPred2 [19], and ATSE [20], and sequences longer
than 50 residues were removed. Non-toxic sequences were retrieved from UniProt
[21] using the query ``NOT KW-0800 AND NOT KW-0020 AND reviewed:true'', and
sequences containing non-standard amino acids or exceeding 50 residues were
excluded. After combining the two groups and removing samples with conflicting
labels, CD-HIT [22] was applied with a sequence-identity threshold of 0.9 to
eliminate redundant sequences, yielding 2{,}138 toxic and 5{,}375 non-toxic
peptides. Following [2], 85\% of the data was used as the training set and the
remaining 15\% as the independent Test1 set, resulting in 1{,}818 toxic and
4{,}569 non-toxic training peptides and 320 toxic and 806 non-toxic Test1
peptides. An additional independent Test2 set, containing 46 toxic and 536
non-toxic peptides, was used to further evaluate generalization ability. In
addition, to assess the model on a strict homology-reduced external benchmark,
we derived an external test set from the independent split of the ToxinPred 3.0
dataset [23] by removing sequences sharing at least 80\% sequence identity with
any training reference (CD-HIT-2D), which retained 977 peptides (471 toxic and
506 non-toxic). Table~1 summarizes the statistics of all datasets used in this
study.
