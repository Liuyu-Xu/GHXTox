     Descriptive structure-quality analysis found an association between mean pLDDT and sub-
group performance in the historical benchmark data. A separate training-OOF comparison found
that hard exclusion of low-confidence samples reduced full-domain performance, supporting the
retained continuous-gating design without establishing pLDDT as a biological mechanism. In
parallel, a Cα-level residue graph compresses chemically complex side chains into single nodes,
whereas a paired five-fold OOF experiment showed that adding the full-atom chemical-site resid-
ual to the frozen backbone increased the BACC point estimate from 0.9153 to 0.9171 and the
MCC point estimate from 0.8312 to 0.8342 (Table 7, bottom rows). These modest internal
point-estimate changes are consistent with complementary chemical information but do not by
themselves establish a general performance gain. Local geometric evidence is meaningful only in
the appropriate sequence context: the same distance or hydrophobic contact can have different
implications depending on residue identity and position, and the model therefore uses pLDDT-
aware cross-attention to let sequence states selectively retrieve spatial evidence, with node- and
peptide-level confidence gating controlling how much structural context is injected (mechanisms
in Section 2.4).
     A single predicted structure provides only one deterministic anchor, whereas short peptides
may exhibit conformational heterogeneity [15]. GHXTox compresses conformer ensembles into
statistics and allows them to produce only a zero-initialized logit residual. On training OOF,
activating the multi-source residual increased MCC from 0.87255 to 0.87445 (paired-bootstrap
∆ = +0.00190, 95% CI [−0.00043, 0.00454], p = 0.203). Under the frozen test protocol, ac-
tivating the residual on the covered records changed the metrics negligibly (Tables 5 and 6:
Test1 MCC 0.8705 to 0.8726 and Test2 MCC 0.6673 to 0.6746, with about one prediction flip on
each benchmark), which is consistent with the decision not to enable conformer statistics during
benchmark inference. This pattern is consistent with, but does not establish, a limitation in
mapping conformational variation to toxicity labels; the present ablation cannot isolate that
possibility from conformer quality, coverage, or residual-model capacity.
