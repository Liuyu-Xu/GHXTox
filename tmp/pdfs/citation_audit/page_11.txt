     Beyond these residue-level corrections, the structural expert additionally allows multi-
conformer statistics to refine the prediction through a logit-level residual [29]. To avoid the
high cost of running the complete geometric network independently on every conformer, GHX-
Tox compresses the ensemble descriptors normalized in Section 2.4.1 into a lightweight statistical
residual: residue-level ensemble features are encoded and pooled with both masked-mean and

of the availability indicator and a learned weight (initialized to 0.25 and regularized by an L1
penalty); when the availability indicator is zero, the residual is exactly zero and the model falls
back to the single-structure prediction. The inference protocol is described in Section 2.5, and
