     A single predicted structure provides a common structural anchor but cannot represent
potential conformational heterogeneity. The conformer-residual stage was therefore trained,
with the single-structure spatial backbone, chemical-site branch, and base classifier frozen, on a
multi-source conformer variant using a 2,795-peptide cohort with up to eight validated, aligned
conformers per peptide. PepFlow is a generalized Boltzmann generator that performs direct
all-atom conformational-ensemble sampling from the peptide energy landscape rather than pre-
dicting a single structure; it has been shown to efficiently recapitulate experimental ensembles
of short linear motifs (SLiMs, typically 3–15 residues) and to generate all-atom conformations
for peptides of different lengths [15]. Short-peptide conformations are therefore provided by
PepFlow. In our pipeline, up to eight conformations are retained per input peptide, and
PepFlow is applied to peptides of at most 15 residues (the actual training cohort spans 11–
15 residues and comprises 761 records with eight conformers each). Longer peptides are pro-
vided by AlphaFold2/ColabFold: AlphaFold2 is a high-accuracy single-structure predictor [13],
and its peptide-structure performance has been systematically evaluated for peptides of 16–60
residues [8], an interval that covers the 16–30-residue cohort; ColabFold, an MSA-accelerated
AlphaFold2 implementation [14], is run in our pipeline with five models and multiple seeds to
generate candidate conformations. These candidates are filtered by an average-pLDDT quality
floor and then reduced to eight diverse conformers per peptide by farthest-point sampling on
Kabsch-aligned Cα-RMSD [25], yielding 2,034 training records. These sources are represented
explicitly in the residual head; nevertheless, generator identity remains partially coupled to
peptide length and is treated as a limitation rather than as an independently identified causal
factor.
     For controlled ablation only, a test-side conformer cache was constructed with fixed source
and sequence-matching policies without using Test1 or Test2 labels; these caches are not used
by the final benchmark predictor or the public inference interface. To remove arbitrary rigid-
body differences and make the statistics comparable across peptides, every admitted ensemble
is normalized before being consumed by the residual branch. An ensemble is represented as
C = {X(1) , . . . , X(M ) }, 1 ≤ M ≤ 8, where X(m) ∈ RL×3 denotes the Cα coordinates of conformer
m. First, each conformer must reproduce the input FASTA sequence residue by residue (both
the Cα sequence and the chemical-site parse are verified); non-matching conformers are rejected,
so all conformers share the same residue-index order without reordering or truncation. Second,
taking the first conformer as the reference, every conformer is centered and aligned to the
reference frame by Kabsch alignment, which obtains an optimal rotation by singular value
decomposition with reflection correction, yielding the aligned coordinates and the per-residue
fluctuation:
