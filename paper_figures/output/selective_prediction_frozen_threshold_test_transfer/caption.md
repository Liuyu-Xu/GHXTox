The training-OOF-derived abstention cutoff transfers without test-specific tuning and identifies lower-risk accepted subsets on both established benchmarks. (A) Applying the same frozen uncertainty-score cutoff (U* = 0.7197239109) yields realized coverages of 88.54% on Test1 (997/1,126) and 86.94% on Test2 (506/582); the dashed 90% line is the training-OOF target used to derive the cutoff, not a Test-set quota. (B) Accepted error decreases from 5.24% to 2.71% on Test1 and from 5.33% to 3.16% on Test2, whereas equal-count random rejection remains at 5.24% and 5.33%, respectively. (C) MCC increases from 0.870 to 0.933 on Test1 and from 0.667 to 0.778 on Test2. Error bars denote 95% intervals from 5,000 label-stratified fixed-mask paired bootstrap replicates; in each replicate the random comparator accepts the same number of resampled observations as the frozen mask. The score feature, preprocessing, binary threshold, and abstention cutoff were fixed from training OOF predictions before Test1/Test2 outcomes were examined. Test labels were used only for post-selection evaluation. These are fixed-policy transfer results on established benchmarks, not validation on a newly collected independent dataset. The MCC axis is restricted to 0.5--1.0 for readability; exact metrics are provided in the companion table.
