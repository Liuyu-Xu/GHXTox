Frozen uncertainty ranking identifies difficult GHXTox predictions more effectively than equal-coverage random rejection on 6,387 training out-of-fold predictions. (A) Accepted-set error and (B) Matthews correlation coefficient at target coverages of 100%, 95%, 90%, 85%, and 80%. The uncertainty score is the direct normalized proximity of the GHXTox fusion probability to the fixed decision threshold (tau = 0.4702225653); it is not a calibrated error probability. Random rejection uses 5,000 uniformly sampled nested rankings with exactly the same accepted count at each coverage. Shaded regions denote 95% intervals from 5,000 class-stratified paired bootstrap replicates; each random-bootstrap evaluation uses one nested random ranking. At the prespecified 90% operating point, accepted error was 2.92% for uncertainty ranking and 5.12% for random rejection; the random-minus-selective difference was 2.19 percentage points (95% paired interval, 1.81--2.57). The toxicity probabilities, fusion rule, and binary threshold were unchanged. Test1 and Test2 labels were not used for policy selection or evaluation; these results quantify internal training-OOF reliability and do not establish external generalization.
