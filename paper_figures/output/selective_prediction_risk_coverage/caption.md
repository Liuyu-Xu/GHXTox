Selective risk as a function of prediction coverage on the 6,387 training out-of-fold predictions. Samples are accepted in ascending order of uncertainty from the frozen cross-fitted selective policy; the underlying toxicity probabilities and binary decision threshold remain unchanged. The amber point marks the prespecified 90% coverage operating point (accepted error, 2.92%; 95% class-stratified bootstrap CI, 2.51-3.39%; 5,000 replicates). The dashed line denotes the full-coverage error (5.17%). This analysis quantifies selective reliability on training OOF predictions and is not an external-validation result.
